I am a PhD student in the Department of Government at Harvard University. I study comparative political economy with a focus on informal and illicit economies, victimization, and contentious politics, primarily within the context of Latin America.

Prior to graduate school, I conducted research at the Urban Institute on crime prevention and victim services in Mexico and the US. I received my BA in political science from Boston College, where I was a Gabelli Presidential Scholar.
